  • Jointly coordinated a research consortium comprising VTT, the Geological Survey of Finland (GTK), and the University of Jyväskylä, managing milestones, cross-team data delivery, and technical reports.
  • Directed code modernization for the pychan3d hydrogeological simulation package, introducing modular data architectures, test suites, and open documentation.
  • Provided technical guidance to research staff on numerical modeling and statistical data interpretation.
Researcher | Uppsala University
Dec 2021 – Dec 2022 | Uppsala, Sweden
  • Micro-to-Macro Subsurface Modeling: Built high-resolution 3D models to evaluate transport pathways and pore-scale connectivity under low-permeability conditions.
  • Data Integration & Sensitivity Pipelines: Developed automated pipelines to integrate structural scan data and borehole logs from the Äspö Hard Rock Laboratory, assessing parameter sensitivities under sparse observations.
  • Technical Reporting: Prepared compliance documentation and technical assessments for Swedish national regulatory review.
Postdoctoral Researcher | Uppsala University
Dec 2019 – Dec 2021 | Uppsala, Sweden
  • Diagnostic Structural Connectivity: Formulated diagnostic methods to quantify spatial heterogeneity and fractional connectivity in discontinuous media.
  • Inverse Modeling & Calibration: Integrated heterogeneous experimental datasets using data-worth analysis to reduce non-uniqueness and improve predictive accuracy.
  • 3D Lattice Algorithmic Optimization: Optimized algorithms to translate 3D structural mapping datasets into computational simulation grids.
Research Associate | IIT Bombay

  • Microscale Multiphysics Modeling: Conducted finite element simulations in COMSOL Multiphysics coupling Navier-Stokes and Darcy-Brinkman fluid mechanics across complex pore and fracture geometries.

Doctoral Researcher | IIT Bombay

Key Research & Technical Projects

VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland

Executed technical evaluations across two dedicated work packages delivering detailed radioactive waste characterisation and technical recommendations on deep repository disposal methods for Norwegian Nuclear Decommissioning.

NND Norway Deep Repository Radioactive Waste

Constitutive & Geotechnical Modeling (SAFER-SAGE)

VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland

Executed constitutive and statistical modeling of cyclic triaxial test data to evaluate non-linear deformation, shear strength, and hydro-mechanical sealing limits of compacted soils under extreme confining stress.

Bentonite Barriers Cyclic Triaxial Constitutive Models

Subsurface Flow & Table Dynamics (GG_LIFECYCLE)

VTT Project Management & Government Grant

Formulated coupled numerical simulations to predict transient rainfall infiltration, groundwater table fluctuations, and long-term drainage performance around containment systems.

Project Manager Transient Infiltration Groundwater Dynamics

Stochastic Network & Transport Modeling (pychan3d / SSM)

Uppsala University & Swedish Radiation Safety Authority

Optimized and refactored the open-source pychan3d library in Python and C++, generating large-scale stochastic channel networks to simulate transport pathways and breakthrough times under sparse data constraints.

pychan3d SSM Sweden Channel Networks

Transient Pressure & Flow-Dimension Analysis

Uppsala University & Äspö Hard Rock Laboratory

Developed an apparent flow-dimension methodology to detect structural anomalies and spatial heterogeneity from noisy, transient hydraulic data streams in crystalline rock.

Äspö HRL Flow Dimension Hydraulic Testing

Soft Computing for Soil Characterization

IIT Bombay

Built artificial neural network (ANN) models to predict the modulus of elasticity and mechanical properties of soils from index testing data, replacing slow empirical correlations with data-driven surrogates.
