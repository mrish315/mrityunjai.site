Senior Hydrogeologist & Subsurface Computational Scientist

Specializing in 3D fractured rock transport modeling, coupled hydro-mechanical (H-M) processes, Discrete Fracture Networks (DFN/CNM), and variably saturated flow in engineered clay barriers and mountain soils.

Core Competencies

Hydrogeology & Transport Modeling

Site-scale Discrete Fracture Networks (DFN), Channel Network Models (CNM), matrix permeability scaling, and contaminant transport pathways in low-permeability granite and complex rock systems.

DFN / CNM pychan3d GoldSim MODFLOW

Geomechanics & Soil Mechanics

Coupled hydro-mechanical (H-M) modeling, matrix suction loss, swelling pressure evolution in bentonite clay barriers (SAFER/SAGE/HAGEN), and rainfall-induced seepage in mountain soils.

COMSOL Multiphysics Richards' Equation Bentonite Barriers Effective Stress

Data Science & Scientific Computing

Developing automated Python pipelines for computational physics, non-linear regression calibration on experimental time-series, and stochastic uncertainty modeling.

Python (NumPy/SciPy) GeoPandas Git / Automation Machine Learning

Subsurface & Hydro-Mechanical Interactive Physics

Demonstration of porous vs. fractured media flow regimes, stress-coupled effective permeability, and variably saturated soil mechanics under transient hydraulic loads.

Research & Professional Experience

Senior Scientist
VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland | May 2023 – Present

Leading numerical flow and contaminant transport modeling in low-permeability rock and engineered barriers for the SAFER, SAGE, and HAGEN safety assessment programs. Expanded the pychan3d Python package and coupled COMSOL multiphysics fields with GoldSim systems models.

Researcher & Postdoctoral Fellow
Uppsala University, Sweden | Dec 2019 – Dec 2022

Evaluated subsurface flow channeling and structural uncertainty across large-scale stochastic fracture networks (9,000–30,000+ fractures) supporting regulatory safety assessments for the Swedish Radiation Safety Authority (SSM).

Doctoral Researcher & Research Associate
IIT Bombay, India | July 2015 – Sept 2019

Formulated dual-porosity hydro-mechanical models to evaluate effective stress changes and matrix deformation. Analyzed geotechnical and hydraulic properties of mountain soils to study slope seepage.
