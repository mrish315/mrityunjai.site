Computes Factor of Safety ($FS$) across user-defined slope geometries, cohesive-frictional soil layers ($c', \phi'$), pore water pressure ($r_u$), and crest surcharges using Ordinary Method of Slices grid search.

KBS-3 DGR Canister Spacing & Thermal Sandbox

Examines thermal decay and plume interference across KBS-3 nuclear waste canisters at -450 m depth as a function of canister/tunnel spacing ($S$), decay age, and rock thermal conductivity ($k$).

Launch Canister Spacing Model

Soil-Atmosphere Carbon & GHG Flux Model
