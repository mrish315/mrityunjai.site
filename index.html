Professional Trajectory

Research & Industrial Experience

Senior Scientist
VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland | May 2023 – Present
  • Led process-based numerical modeling of fluid flow, solute transport, and environmental safety risks in low-permeability rock and engineered barrier caps.
  • Developed customized Python packages to convert complex 3D fracture networks into calculation matrices and integrated radionuclide transport models with GoldSim.
  • Analyzed experimental datasets for the SAFER, SAGE, and HAGEN projects in COMSOL Multiphysics (Richards' equation) to model transient moisture and swelling dynamics.
Researcher
Uppsala University, Sweden | Dec 2021 – Dec 2022
  • Developed predictive tracer transport and subsurface breakthrough models supporting safety assessment programs funded by the Swedish Radiation Safety Authority (SSM).
  • Designed automated long-term simulation pipelines to run anomaly and sensitivity scenarios for fluid migration in deep geological formations.
  • Processed dense site characterization datasets from Äspö Hard Rock Laboratory (HRL) to map breakthrough arrival times under high data uncertainty.
Postdoctoral Researcher
Uppsala University, Sweden | Dec 2019 – Dec 2021
  • Pioneered a novel well-testing methodology utilizing long-term apparent flow-dimension analysis to evaluate subsurface media heterogeneity.
  • Refactored and optimized the pychan3d Python computational library to programmatically translate 3D Discrete Fracture Networks into Channel Network Models.
  • Cross-calibrated high-noise hydraulic datasets (dilution, pumping, tracer) with international multidisciplinary research teams.
Doctoral Researcher & Research Associate
IIT Bombay, India | July 2015 – Sept 2019
  • Investigated non-linear flow behavior through rock systems, evaluating critical Reynolds and Forchheimer number data transitions.
  • Formulated dual-porosity math models to simulate fluid exchange dynamics between matrix pores and fracture planes.
  • Analyzed shear strength, weathering, and rainfall-induced slope seepage in mountain soils.
