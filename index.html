Core Competencies

Hydrogeology & Fracture Networks

2D and 3D Discrete Fracture Networks (DFN), Channel Network Modeling (CNM), matrix permeability scaling, and solute/tracer transport pathways in heterogeneous low-permeability formations.

Coupled Geomechanics & Barriers

Coupled hydro-mechanical (H-M) processes, pore-pressure evolution, swelling pressure, and transient variably saturated flow through compacted bentonite clay barriers and soil profiles.

Career History

Research Experience

Senior Scientist
VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland | May 2023 – Dec 2025
  • Led numerical flow and solute transport modeling in low-permeability rock and engineered barrier caps for national safety assessment frameworks (SAFER, SAGE, HAGEN).
  • Developed customized Python packages to convert 3D fracture networks into calculation matrices and coupled finite-element output with GoldSim.
  • Evaluated transient moisture movement and swelling pressure evolution in compacted bentonite using COMSOL Multiphysics (Richards' equation).
Researcher
Uppsala University, Sweden | Dec 2021 – Dec 2022
  • Developed predictive tracer transport models for deep geological repository safety programs funded by the Swedish Radiation Safety Authority (SSM).
  • Constructed stochastic simulation pipelines to analyze breakthrough pathways and arrival times under high subsurface uncertainty.
  • Processed dense hydrogeological logging data from the Äspö Hard Rock Laboratory (HRL).
Postdoctoral Researcher
Uppsala University, Sweden | Dec 2019 – Dec 2021
  • Pioneered a long-term apparent flow-dimension analysis method to map subsurface media heterogeneity.
  • Refactored and optimized the open-source pychan3d Python package to translate Discrete Fracture Networks into Channel Network Models.
  • Cross-calibrated pumping, dilution, and tracer experiment datasets across international research consortiums.
Doctoral Researcher & Research Associate
IIT Bombay, India | July 2015 – Sept 2019
  • Formulated dual-porosity hydro-mechanical models to evaluate matrix-fracture fluid exchange dynamics.
  • Investigated non-linear flow transitions in rock discontinuities using experimental data regression and numerical sensitivity analysis.
  • Evaluated shear strength and rainfall-induced seepage in mountain soils.
