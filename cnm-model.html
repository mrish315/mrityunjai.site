Graph-Based FDM Hydraulic Solver Mechanics

In this custom graph builder, non-boundary internal junction nodes satisfy Mass Conservation ($\sum Q_{ij} = 0$). Steady-state hydraulic heads ($H_i$) are calculated by solving the graph Laplacian system using a converging Gauss-Seidel iterative solver:

Hi = [ Σ (Cij · Hj) ] / [ Σ Cij ]

Channel flow flux is evaluated as $Q_{ij} = C_{ij}(H_i - H_j)$. Advective solute tracer particles are continuously injected at active Inlet nodes ($H > 0$), routing probabilistically through outgoing channels weighted by flux magnitude ($Q_{ij}$).
