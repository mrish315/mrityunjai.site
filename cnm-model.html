Graph-Based FDM Hydraulic Solver & Steady-State Mechanics

In this graph solver, non-boundary internal junction nodes satisfy Mass Conservation (Σ Qij = 0). Steady-state hydraulic heads (Hi) are calculated by solving the graph system using a converging Gauss-Seidel iterative solver:

Hi = [ Σ (Cij · Hj) ] / [ Σ Cij ]

Channel conductances (Cij) range from 1.0 × 10-12 to 1.0 m²/s (default: 1.0 × 10-5 m²/s). Flow rate flux is evaluated as Qij = Cij (Hi - Hj) in m³/s. When toggled ON, advective solute transport particles move proportionally to local channel flow velocities.
