KBS-3 DGR Safety Concept

High-level nuclear waste is encapsulated in copper-cast iron canisters and deposited in vertical holes at a depth of $-400\text{ m}$ to $-500\text{ m}$ inside crystalline granite rock. The canisters are buffered by compacted bentonite clay. Decay heat dissipation $Q(t) = Q_0 e^{-\lambda t}$ dictates minimum canister spacing $S$ to prevent thermal degradation of bentonite ($T_{\text{max}} < 95^\circ\text{C}$).
