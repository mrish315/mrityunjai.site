Richards' Equation Framework

Variably saturated flow through engineered soil caps and clay barriers is modeled using Richards' equation: $\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial t} = \nabla \cdot \left( K(\psi) \nabla (\psi + z) \right)$. Matrix suction loss and wetting front propagation are controlled by non-linear soil-water retention curves (SWRC).
